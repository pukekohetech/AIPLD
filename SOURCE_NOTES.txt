The toolkit uses selected English-medium NZC Years 9–10 content for suggested teaching connections. Learning Languages uses proficiency stages; the French example is Novice 3. The toolkit does not provide a mapping of the full NZC, Te Marautanga o Aotearoa, senior subjects or NCEA standards.

Curriculum knowledge and practices in the toolkit are paraphrased summaries. Teaching examples, prompts, LI and SC are teacher-designed applications. They are not Ministry-endorsed lesson plans. One short activity does not show coverage of a whole teaching sequence.
